Lumipop — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Developer: Kwansik Nam · Contact: kwansiknam@gmail.com

Lumipop is a puzzle game. We want you to play without worrying about your data. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. There are no accounts.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, daily challenge results, settings (such as sound), and whether you bought "Remove ads" are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Gameplay analytics

To make the game better, the app sends anonymous gameplay events (for example: a game started, a score, how long a round lasted, which button was tapped) to our analytics provider, PostHog. Each install gets a random identifier that is not linked to your identity. We also send the app version and platform (iOS or Android). We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used for approximate location), and device information. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with the "Privacy" button on the title screen.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Buying "Remove ads" turns off ads between games. Optional ads that you choose to watch (for example, to continue a round) remain available.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We only learn whether "Remove ads" is owned so we can turn ads off.

Children

Lumipop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad personalization: use the "Privacy" button (EEA/UK), decline tracking on iOS, or reset your advertising ID on Android.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app.
  • Ask a question or request deletion of analytics data: email kwansiknam@gmail.com with the date you installed the app. Because the data is anonymous, we may need details to find it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
